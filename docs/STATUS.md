Dropped by the owner 2026-10-15; deleted from the tree the same day (`1ac78f8`, `011b837`).  What survives
and was worth the line: the frozen trajectory interface (`PLAN.md` §4.1) -- a planner arrived behind it and

  re-projection of a plan onto the vehicle, `/planner/stalled`.  DELETED: the "start within 0.50 m of the
  vehicle" gate (`19766bc`) -- the refusal, not the far start, starved three sorties.

  `local_data_.position_traj_` from `t_cur` and never reads `start_pt`, so `spline(0) = old_traj(t_cur)` --
  where the OLD PLAN believes the vehicle is.  Anchored by `0013`/`7412be3`;
  `docs/metrics/phaseB-AP8-anchor-20261015.txt`.

  (`t_cur > duration - 1e-2` -> WAIT_TARGET), so a vehicle slower than `manager/max_vel` makes the planner
  declare arrival and stop planning (`0010`/`475342f`; `docs/metrics/ego-offline-lag-ab.txt`).
* **The crash defects**: `0002` -- a goal before the first odometry segfaults, a `spin_some` in a callback
  aborts the node, the goal's altitude is discarded; `std::bad_alloc` killed the node 35 s into AP5 with
  nothing watching the children; the stall escalation died of `NameError: name 'Bool' is not defined`
  until `7a00430`.
* **The pool-exhaustion finding**: `[ERROR] [Coord2Index]: Ran out of pool, index=88 100 25,
  POOL_SIZE=100 100 100` with 10468 x `AstarSearch: Unable to handle the initial or end point` and 10167 x
  `check_collision_and_rebound: ERROR! the drone is in obstacle` -- the map pool is exhausted, so the A* and
  the ESDF that should do the avoiding error instead and the FSM cycles EMERGENCY_STOP <-> GEN_NEW_TRAJ
