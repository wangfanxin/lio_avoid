================================================================================
1. WHAT WAS CHANGED, AND WHY THESE THREE NUMBERS AND NOT ONE
================================================================================
One physical change: the noise the EKF is told about our position source.
  VISO_POS_M_NSE   0.03 -> 0.01   (30 mm -> 10 mm; the LIO is measured at 3.38-4.57 mm, A1)
That setting is a FLOOR, not a value: `libs/AP_VisualOdom/AP_VisualOdom_MAV.cpp:42` does
`posErr = constrain_float(posErr, _frontend.get_pos_noise(), 100.0f)`, and our ODOMETRY carries
`[0.0]*21, [0.0]*21` covariances (`bridge/mavlink_impl.py:442-449`), so the message declares zero error
and the floor is what the EKF uses.  The flight log proves the chain: `PErr` was a constant 30.00 mm
before and is a constant 10.00 mm now.

WARNING, FROM THIS PROJECT'S OWN RECORD: EK3_POSNE_M_NSE / EK3_VELNE_M_NSE are the **GPS** noises
(`AP_NavEKF3.cpp:184/:158`) and EK3_ALT_M_NSE the **baro** noise (:218); with EK3_SRC1_*=6 (ExternalNav)
they are inert.  The parameters that actually carry our measurement noise are the VISO_* trio, and with
VISO_TYPE 1 they reach the EKF through AP_VisualOdom.  An earlier round believed otherwise -- see the
header of the frozen file -- which is why the frozen file's 0.03 / 0.03 / 0.10 lines changed nothing.

Two couplings moved with it, both to hold the ABSOLUTE tolerances where the frozen file put them:
  FS_EKF_THRESH   10.0 -> 30.0    the failsafe compares the innovation consistency TEST RATIO
                                  (`AP_AHRS::get_variances` -> `NavEKF3::getVariances`, "innovation
                                  consistency test ratios", AP_NavEKF3.cpp:1532), which scales as
                                  1/noise.  The frozen file records the cost of getting this wrong:
                                  "with 3.0 the tolerance was 90 mm and a normal trajectory-following
                                  manoeuvre tripped it -- 3 of 5 Mission B flights ended in LAND mode".
                                  0.300 m / 0.01 = 30.
  EK3_POS_I_GATE  500 -> 1500     "percentage number of standard deviations applied to the position
                                  innovation consistency check" (AP_NavEKF3.cpp:186-192, 500 = 5.00 SD),
                                  so it also scales with the noise: 5 SD x 30 mm was 150 mm absolute and
                                  5 SD x 10 mm would have been 50 mm, i.e. 3x tighter in metres -- the
                                  way good measurements start being rejected.  Kept at 150 mm absolute.

================================================================================
5. WHAT THIS RUN DOES NOT FIX, AND THE NEXT EXPERIMENT (measured, not guessed)
================================================================================
* THE VELOCITY FEED IS A STAIRCASE, and this is the next single-variable experiment.
  Measured from the logs: VISV arrives at 48.40 Hz but carries only 10.08 DISTINCT value tuples/s -- the
  10 Hz `/lio/odom` source held by the FSM into a 48 Hz stream -- with steps at each change of median
  10.41 mm/s, p90 134.73 mm/s, max 287.28 mm/s, while we declare the same velocity to the EKF as 30 mm/s.
  A 135 mm/s step declared as 30 mm/s noise is a ~4.5 sigma innovation every 100 ms.  Its honest noise is
  LARGER than 0.03, not smaller, which is why VISO_VEL_M_NSE was left alone here: changing it in the same
  run would have made this run's result unattributable.  Meanwhile the FCU's velocity estimate got worse
  (N 13.66 -> 36.76 mm/s) which is consistent with the staircase, not with the position change.
  NOTE: the velocity channel has no covariance path at all -- `GCS_MAVLINK::handle_odometry`
  (GCS_Common.cpp:4052) reads pose_covariance for posErr/angErr but calls
  `handle_vision_speed_estimate(...)` with the SPEED estimate, and EKF3 takes the velocity noise from
  `_frontend.get_vel_noise()` (`AP_VisualOdom_MAV.cpp:79`).  So the source cannot declare its own velocity
  accuracy; the parameter or the source RATE is the only lever.
* THE HOVER'S ONE MISS IS NOT A TIMING ACCIDENT.  c3 was held 5.8 s by the FSM and declared ARRIVED by the
  producer at 0.055 m, but `dwell-audit` sees only 4.80 s inside 0.15 m because the arrival RING carried
  the aircraft outside the judging radius and back, splitting one visit in two (the same mechanism as
  RECT11's c3, 4.96 s).  The fix is to start the dwell clock on SETTLED (inside the radius AND slow), not
  on the first crossing -- scheduled, not done.
* THE VERTICAL DATUM IS NOT NOISE and no parameter will remove it: across all eight runs with an FCU log
  the in-flight vertical datum offset is -0.087 +- 0.003 m and the parked value -0.110 +- 0.004 m.
* ONE SORTIE IS NOT A RESULT.  RECT21 is a single flight and needs a repeat before section 3 is a
  conclusion; the baseline RECT7 is likewise one flight of eight with an FCU log, and all eight sit in
  16.18-21.19 mm horizontally, which is the band this run moved out of.
