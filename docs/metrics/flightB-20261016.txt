SO THE PACKAGE SPLIT IN TWO, CLEANLY
  * THE TRACKING HALF WORKED.  The reference's largest step fell from 0.99 m to 0.36 m -- exactly what the clamp
    predicted -- and the tracking error's tail fell with it (p95 0.51 -> 0.36, max 0.92 -> 0.39 m; at the 1.5-2.0
    m/s band 0.346 -> 0.133 m).  C6 and C7 both did what they were supposed to do, and C2/C3 fired for the first
    time: the aircraft's own position being inside the map is now VISIBLE (28 lines) where before it was silent.
  * THE PLAN HALF REGRESSED, AND THE MECHANISM IS IN THE TABLE: `clr_m`'s minimum went from +0.048 m to
    **-0.245 m with 148 negative plans**, i.e. the planner is now committing trajectories that go THROUGH pillars
    -- and the figure shows the consequence: four separate penetrations along the leg-2 corridor instead of one
    transient excursion.  The two changes whose observable is the plan are exactly the two that regressed: C4
    (the whole-spline acceptance test and control-point scan) and C5 (the A* clearance cost).
    The likely mechanism is the one the planner audit already documented as latent (its B1): when the optimiser
    REJECTS a trajectory, the segment is ERASED and the function still returns TRUE
    (`bspline_optimizer.cpp:1403-1409,1510-1511`), so a stricter acceptance test does not produce a safer plan --
    it produces MORE rejections, and the trajectory that gets committed has segments with no repulsion at all.
    That hypothesis is falsifiable and cheap to test: revert C4+C5 and the plan's `clr_m` should return to the
    positive side.
  * AND C2 IS TOO EAGER.  28 vehicle-in-obstacle events produced 26 EMERGENCY_STOP transitions: the map marks the
    aircraft's own position often enough that "hold position" became a recurring state.  The bench caught the
    first version of this (one tick was enough); the flight says the gates now stop the single-tick false
    positive but not the underlying disagreement between where the map thinks the aircraft is and where it is.

     right way, and the two diagnostics that now work.

  4. Only after that, the margin work (C4/C5) done properly: the audit's A4 shows the A* cost is the right lever
     but its single previous flight and this one both say the rejection paths around it are not ready.
