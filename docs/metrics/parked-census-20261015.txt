=== WHY THIS SHAPE ============================================================================
DIAG4 (in flight) could not settle (A) 'the map was empty where the pillars are' vs (B) 'the map had
them and the planner ignored them', because the cloud recorder kept ~0.5% of the relay's output: 25792
points where the relay published millions.  A census taken from that sample cannot tell a missing
pillar from a missed message.  So this run:
   * the vehicle is PARKED -- no arming, no supervisor, no plan box, nothing to race the censuses;
   * three censuses run at once, one per STAGE, so a loss is LOCALISED rather than merely detected:
       /lio/cloud_world                        the LIO's registered map
       /ego/cloud                              what OUR RELAY published from it
       /drone_0_grid/grid_map/occupancy_inflate what EGO'S MAP made of that
   * each subscribes RELIABLE at depth 50 (what the publishers offer), keeps a running UNION of
     occupied cells with numpy on the raw buffer, prints its verdict every 15 s, and terminates ITSELF.

=== WHAT IS ESTABLISHED, AND WHAT IS NOT ======================================================
ESTABLISHED: (1) our relay is faithful -- it loses nothing between the LIO and ego; (2) ego's map is
where the pillars are lost, losing 11 of 34 pillars and 62% of the distinct occupied cells; (3) the
loss has a clean spatial structure (a half-space through the vehicle).
/mnt/d/distill_self/_stage/make-census-evidence.sh: line 56: local_update_range: command not found
NOT ESTABLISHED: the mechanism.  The leading hypothesis --  tested against a wrong
/mnt/d/distill_self/_stage/make-census-evidence.sh: line 57: camera_pos_: command not found
or offset  -- is a hypothesis, and the next round tests it directly by reading the map's
own position against the odometry it is fed.
ALSO NOT ESTABLISHED, and it is a limit of this instrument: THE CENSUS IS z-BLIND.  It reconstructs a
2-D union of x,y, so a cell counted as 'on the pillar' could in principle come from another height
(the ground plane under it, or the ceiling).  The three-way comparison is unaffected -- all three
stages were censused the same way -- but a per-pillar claim should not be read as a 3-D statement.
