  i.e. ego stayed on the target and kept replanning, and the FSM's new DIAG named its refusal every

THE ONE REMAINING MECHANISM (next step, in order)
  1. THE ADAPTER RE-PROJECTS AN EXPIRED SPLINE INTO A 2-POSE STUB.  Its own line, repeated ~250 times:
       "published 2 poses on /planner/traj  (t_cur 0.415 / 0.415 s, start 0.1392 m from the vehicle,
        min pillar clearance 0.334 m, traj dt 0.0148 s, arc step 0.0078 m)"
     t_cur has already reached duration, so the "remaining" plan is the spline's last instant: 2 poses
     0.006 m apart (traj.csv: n=2 arc_m=0.005824, points (0.015985,-4.115627) and (0.016943,-4.109883),
     both ~0.01 m from the vehicle).  The FSM refuses every one of them on --min-traj-m 0.10 and the
     sortie aborts 60 s later.  ego committed no new spline after traj 8 (bsplines 8 for the whole
     flight, the last at t = 75.0 s), so there is nothing else to publish.
     The FSM' own answer for this case is already upstream's: when the plan's clock is out, hover at the
     last point -- but here the last point is where the vehicle already is, so the mission cannot move.
     The interface's job is to say "I have no plan" (a stale/empty publish is not a plan), loudly and
     once, rather than to spam a 6 mm stub 250 times and let the consumer guess.
  2. THE GOAL SOURCE DISAGREES WITH ITSELF AT THE END.  The adapter's last received goal is
     "goal W[+1.5918 +3.6696 +2.0000] -> L[+3.6723 -1.5958 +1.8844]  round trip 0.000000 mm" (the
     sequencer's stop 2, published 41 times), while ego's own end_pt_ stayed at [0.001 -4.001 1.884] --
     stop 1's goal -- as the last two GOAL-TRACE lines show (have_target=1, then have_target=0, odom
     [0.023 -4.128 1.786]).  So the new goal was received and converted but never reached ego's
     waypointCallback, and the 1 Hz re-forward kept re-sending stop 1.  `_goal_src` has two writers
     (the sequencer's latched goal and the /mission/stop-index-derived goal) -- that is the last of the
     handshake-shaped layers and it should be reduced to ONE source, the sequencer's.
  3. Only then is the 3 m/s / corridor-width question worth re-measuring.
