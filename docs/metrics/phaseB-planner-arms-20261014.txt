  * C1 (final point) 0.007-0.029 m in every grid-0.05 arm; A1 2.8-4.7 mm; A2 3.4-4.6 mm; A3 PASS;
    A4 11.1-24.9 mm.  The estimate, the follower and the airframe all pass.
  * The adapter's gate is what keeps C2 clean: in the grid-0.05 arms 0 of ~90 000 airborne samples ever
    entered the 0.20 m footprint (min clearance +0.28 .. +0.34 m) because every illegal plan was refused.
    In the two arms where an illegal plan DID get through (K1, P1) the aircraft itself went inside.

WHAT THE PLAN SIDE HAS ESTABLISHED (this is the whole remaining problem)
  1. The refusals are DEEP penetrations, not near misses: -0.14 .. -0.34 m inside a 0.40 m pillar, while every
     PUBLISHED plan held the contract (L1: min 0.278 m, median 0.350 m over 51 plans).
  2. The pillar is NOT unseen and NOT forgotten.  tools/ego-map-audit.py (round 9): all ten O1 refusals cut
     the SAME pillar (#52 at W 3.76, 0.81 -- the one 0.85 m from stop 1), and ego's own
     grid_map/occupancy_inflate holds 18363 cells within 0.15 m of its shell in 368 of 368 scans, 7919 of
     them in the plan's own altitude band z 1.70-2.05, in 367 of 368 scans.
  3. ego's own checks say the plan is fine: refine_success 121 vs 4, plan_success 121 vs 4,
     "First 3 control points in obstacles" 0, "the drone is in obstacle" 0.
  4. The shape is a DIVE AND RETURN (round 10, the clearance profile of refused traj 12): control points 0-3
     sit at the vehicle with 0.27-0.45 m of clearance, 5-8 dive to -0.307 m inside pillar #52 at 0.6-1.2 m
     from the plan's own start, 9-14 come back out and continue legally to stop 2.
  5. The vehicle itself had 0.467 m of room on that side.  So the plan had room to stay legal and did not.
  6. dist0 = 0.60 m is a SOFT cost and is violated by 0.31 m; the hard margin in ego's map is the 0.10 m
     inflation, which is SMALLER than our 0.20 m contract.  A soft cost cannot enforce a hard contract.
  7. lambda_collision 2.0 halves the penetration (-0.307 -> -0.144 m) and cuts refusals 13 -> 4, but the
     optimizer destabilises ("First 3 control points in obstacles" 136, refine_success=0 274) and the
     aircraft then clips a pillar itself (C2 3.34 % under).
  8. dist0 0.25 changes nothing (13 refusals, -0.232 m).  p_hit 0.90 changes nothing.  A 15 s accumulated map
     makes everything worse (M1).  A finer grid (0.05) makes the aircraft side cleaner but not the plans.

THE DEADLOCK'S LAST LINK (round 11, Q1): the legal prefix of a refused plan is 0.20-0.39 m, and the adapter
refused prefixes shorter than --min-prefix-m 0.30 m, so the vehicle could not move AT ALL.  Allowing 0.15 m
produced 14 truncations -- the aircraft moved (C1 0.012 m) and the refusals fell 13 -> 4 -- but the plan
stream then starved on ego's own start gate instead ("starts 0.853 / 0.865 / 3.450 m from the vehicle
(> 0.50 m)"), i.e. ego kept offering plans anchored where the vehicle no longer was.
