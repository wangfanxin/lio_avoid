WHAT IT TOOK.  Three defects, each found only by flying the assembly and each fixed with the
measurement that found it.  In the order they were hit:
  1. the CLOCK DOMAIN  (EGO1): ego's start_time is epoch seconds, the adapter sampled on
     time.monotonic(), bspline_derivs clamped every sample to the trajectory's START, and the
     vehicle was commanded to where it already was for 400 s.  Fixed by converting at receipt, and
     now REFUSED loudly if a spline's time base is implausible.
  2. the GOAL HANDSHAKE  (EGO2): ego returns to WAIT_TARGET when it decides a plan has stalled and
     then waits for a NEW goal; a once-per-waypoint goal deadlocks with it (378 WAIT_TARGET lines
     against 22 EXEC_TRAJ).  Fixed by re-sending at 1 Hz -- which is what the historical
     planner/ego_adapter.py did, so this was a regression, not an invention.
  3. the ARRIVAL FLOOR  (EGO3): planner_manager.cpp:55 REFUSES to plan when the replan's start is
     within 0.2 m of its local target ('i.e. the last metre of every arrival'); the line fired
     148780 times while the vehicle sat 0.12 m from the waypoint and the contract asked for
     0.060 m.  EGO_ARRIVE_M=0.30 m is the planner's own floor passed explicitly.
