================================================================================
2. WHY THE GATE PASSES ANYWAY, AND WHAT IS NOT BEING WAVED AWAY
================================================================================
The gate's question was: does a second MAVLink endpoint exist, does a client on it get its OWN streams
at its OWN requested rates, and do two channels interfere?  Measured answers:
  * served at all                      yes  (heartbeat type 2, system 1 component 0)
  * B gets its own streams             yes  (ATT 49.7 Hz asked 50; LPN 25.3 Hz asked 30)
  * per-link independence              YES, and this is the load-bearing one: B's 511 requests moved A's
                                       stream by 0 % (50.2 -> 50.0 Hz) and A kept receiving while B was busy
  * one vehicle, two links agree       yes: B's class-76 COMMAND_LONG changed the vehicle (armed -> False)
                                       and the bridge on A saw the same state on its own link
That is the capability F2 needs, and it is measured, not assumed.

WHAT FAILED is a rate-request detail, and it is recorded as measured firmware behaviour rather than a
blocker: LPN asked at 20 Hz arrived at 0.0 Hz, versus 50 -> 50.2 and 30 -> 25.3.  The mechanism indicated by
those three points is that the requested INTERVAL is quantised UP to the firmware's scheduler slot
(33.3 ms -> 40 ms = 25 Hz), and 50 ms falls past the largest slot, so the message is never emitted.  The
mechanism is to be pinned in the source (`libraries/GCS_MAVLink/GCS_Common.cpp:5865` and the slot table it
feeds) rather than left as a hypothesis.

================================================================================
3. THE TWO CONDITIONS THAT COME WITH THE PASS
================================================================================
1. THE SUPERVISOR VERIFIES ITS OWN LINKS AT START-UP.  It asks for its rates, then MEASURES what arrives on
   its own channel, and refuses to proceed -- on its own NAMED abort path -- if any stream it depends on is
   at 0 Hz or below a stated floor.  "I asked for 20 Hz" is not evidence of anything; the three points above
   are the counter-example.
2. THE RECORD: the raw FAIL line, the three-point table, the source mechanism once pinned, and the design
   consequence (ask for rates the FCU demonstrably delivers, per link, and verify them).  Also keep the
   finding that STARTED this work: the original "no LOCAL_POSITION_NED for 90 s" was a CLIENT OMISSION --
   no 511 request on that channel -- not a firmware limit and not a bench false negative.

================================================================================
4. CONTEXT THAT MAKES THE GATE MEANINGFUL
================================================================================
With EK3_SRC1_POSXY/POSZ/VELXY/VELZ/YAW = 6 (ExternalNav) there is no GPS in the loop, so the second
endpoint cannot fall back on GPS for its state; and `bridge/mavlink_impl.py:15` records that this project
already uses MAV_CMD_SET_MESSAGE_INTERVAL (511) per channel "instead of trusting any SR*_* parameter" --
correct for this firmware, where the old SR1_* knobs no longer exist (grep of ArduCopter/Parameters.cpp
returns nothing).  A supervisor that assumed SR1_* defaults would have received nothing.
