MISSION B, THE CIRCLE: THE PLAN-SUPPLY LOOP, MEASURED END TO END (2026-10-14)
================================================================================
Every number here comes from a sortie of the same mission (12 stops on the r = 4 m circle), the same
aircraft (SITL_MODEL=x against FRAME_TYPE 1), the same LIO and the same 0.20 m hard gate.

1. THE AIRCRAFT SIDE IS DONE.
   C1 0.003-0.029 m, A1 2.5-9.6 mm, A2 3.4-4.3 mm, A3 PASS, A4 12-49 mm, and C2 is 0 of ~86 000 samples
   inside the 0.20 m footprint in every sortie whose stations the planner could serve at all.

2. THE THREE SORTIES THAT DIFFER ONLY IN THE WAIT-LOOP HOLD.
     hold policy                          trajectories flown   stops      C2 min clearance / below 0.20 m
     AF1  always at the last endpoint     20                   8 of 12    -0.376 m / 46.6 % (14.03 % INSIDE a pillar)
     AG1  only within 0.50 m              1                    1 of 12    +0.340 m / 0 of 87 032
     AH1  gated on the segment's clearance 1                   1 of 12    +0.283 m / 0 of 86 479
   AF1 finished the sequence and landed; AG1/AH1 are legal and starve.  So the mission advances only while
   the VEHICLE MOVES, and the only motion the FSM can create on its own is that pull -- which is illegal at a
   distance and has nothing legal to pull toward up close (offline: a 0.3 m segment beside stop 1 measures
   +0.208 m and is allowed; a 0.8 m segment toward pillar #52 measures -0.339 m and is refused).

3. WHERE THE PLANS COME FROM (a correction to the earlier model).
   ego's OWN committed trajectories are NOT anchored at the vehicle: MEASURED over AF1's 79 commits,
   |cp0 - truth| median 4.711 m, p95 7.285 m, max 7.746 m, and 0 of 79 within 0.50 m.  The plan that gets
   flown is the ADAPTER's re-projection of that spline onto the vehicle ("publish from the point nearest the
   vehicle"), which is why the aircraft can fly plans whose raw start is metres away.  Any future work on
   "make ego anchor at the vehicle" must not break that re-projection.
