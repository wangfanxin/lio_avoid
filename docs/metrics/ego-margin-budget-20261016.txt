=== WHY THE MARGIN WAS PUSHED AT ALL ==========================================================
The C2 row asks for **no sample within 0.20 m of a pillar surface**.  After the relay window removed the
penetration (truth minimum -0.164 m -> +0.150 and +0.056 m over two runs), the margin was the remaining gap, and
`grid_map/obstacles_inflation` is the only HARD margin that map has -- its distance terms are a swarm separation
cost that is empty for one vehicle, and it has no ESDF and no distance query at all.

**TWO CELLS ARE NOT BETWEEN ONE AND THREE -- THERE IS NO VALUE BETWEEN THEM.**  `inf_step =
ceil(obstacles_inflation / 0.1)`, so 0.100 and 0.099 are one cell and two cells, and nothing in between exists.
Two cells is catastrophically worse than three in the error counts, and the reason is the interaction with the
previous round's fix: **the relay window already thickens the map** (it unions recent scans, which is what makes
the map hold an obstacle through the approach), so the inflation budget has to SHRINK, not grow.  The two fixes
are not independent, and this round measured the sign of the interaction by breaking it.

So the launch file is back to 0.099 and carries the whole budget beside the parameter, and **the map cannot
deliver the C2 row's 0.20 m footprint.**

=== WHAT IS LEFT, AND IT IS NOT THE MAP =======================================================
  * `optimization/lambda_fitness` (1.0) is the only optimiser weight that pulls the B-spline onto the A* path;
    `lambda_collision` (0.5) and `dist0` (0.5) multiply the SWARM separation term, which is empty for a single
    vehicle (`bspline_optimizer.cpp:962-1001`), so raising them is a no-op.
  * `check_collision_and_rebound()` checks only the first 2/3 of a trajectory (`i_end = end_idx -
    (end_idx - order_)/3`) and, when its rebound A* fails, DROPS the segment and keeps the colliding trajectory
    (`:1405-1408`) -- a hard check with a soft failure mode.
  * and the honest alternative: state that the C2 row's 0.20 m footprint is not reachable by this planner at this
    map resolution, with the measurement above as the reason, rather than keep tuning until a lucky run passes.

=== THE NEXT ACTION, PREPARED AND DELIBERATELY NOT APPLIED (`_stage/apply-collision-coverage1.py`) ======
The last four rounds' measurements converge on one line of code.  `check_collision_and_rebound()` is the only
mechanism in this planner that can push a trajectory out of an obstacle, and at a relay window of 8 s or more the
counters show it firing **2.2-5.1 MILLION times per crossing**, finding **15 000-38 000 collisions**, with its
rebound A* succeeding essentially every time (`astar_fail` 0-161).  Seventeen thousand successful recoveries, and
still **0 of 25 crossings meet the C2 row** -- so what is left UNCOVERED is the suspect, and the function names it:

    int i_end = end_idx - (end_idx - order_) / 3;   // only the first TWO-THIRDS are ever swept

plus the case where a colliding segment's rebound A* fails: that segment is DROPPED and the trajectory kept
(`bspline_optimizer.cpp:1405-1408`).

The prepared patch extends that sweep to the whole trajectory and changes nothing else -- not the map, not what A*
is offered, not the optimiser's costs.  It is deliberately NOT applied: the flight tree stands at the verified
documented configuration, and an applied-but-unbuilt, unflown patch is exactly the state this project has spent
rounds learning to avoid.  Prediction recorded BEFORE it is flown: the plan's worst point stops being one the map
marks OCCUPIED (the failure round 21 measured at window 8) and the clearance population improves; the refutation
is no change in either, which would mean the uncovered third is not where the penetrations live either.

=== PATCH COLLISION-COVERAGE/1, FLOWN AND REFUTED (`DIAG26-coverage1`) ==================================
The prepared patch (extend the collision sweep from the first two-thirds of a trajectory to all of it) was
applied, built (`bspline_opt` 17.2 s, clean) and flown at the best arm's window (8 s):

**Refuted, on the prediction written before the flight** ("the plan's worst point stops being one the map marks

deeper than either window-8 baseline crossing and did not even complete cleanly (rc=1), so extending the sweep to

What that leaves standing, stated exactly: the recovery machinery fires 2.2-5.1 M times per crossing and succeeds
essentially always; extending its coverage changes nothing measurable; the map's own margin cannot be raised

exceeds every effect tried, parameters and code alike.  **Three code/parameter routes have now been flown and all
three are refuted**: the A* clearance cost (-0.057 m then a geofence abort), raising the map's inflation (twice),
and this coverage extension.  The patch is REVERTED and the library rebuilt, so the flight tree is back at the
