=== WHY THE MARGIN WAS PUSHED AT ALL ==========================================================
The C2 row asks for **no sample within 0.20 m of a pillar surface**.  After the relay window removed the
penetration (truth minimum -0.164 m -> +0.150 and +0.056 m over two runs), the margin was the remaining gap, and
`grid_map/obstacles_inflation` is the only HARD margin that map has -- its distance terms are a swarm separation
cost that is empty for one vehicle, and it has no ESDF and no distance query at all.

=== AND IT IS NOW EXHAUSTED, AT BOTH ENDS =====================================================
    1 cell (0.099 m, upstream's own value)   the crossing clears every pillar; margin +0.056 .. +0.150 m
    2 cells (0.200 m, DIAG12, just flown)    **1 030 645** `a star error`
                                             **1 058 710** `the drone is in obstacle`
                                             2032 `Failed to generate direction`, a **646 MB** planner log
                                             truth minimum -0.031 m, 90.2% of samples inside the 0.20 m band
                                             the 420 s WATCHDOG killed the sortie (rc=143, hung=1)
    3 cells (0.300 m, DIAG7)                 2789 `a star error`, 316 670 'terminal point in obstacle',
                                             the vehicle flew through the field to 0.1 m altitude

**TWO CELLS ARE NOT BETWEEN ONE AND THREE -- THERE IS NO VALUE BETWEEN THEM.**  `inf_step =
ceil(obstacles_inflation / 0.1)`, so 0.100 and 0.099 are one cell and two cells, and nothing in between exists.
Two cells is catastrophically worse than three in the error counts, and the reason is the interaction with the
previous round's fix: **the relay window already thickens the map** (it unions recent scans, which is what makes
the map hold an obstacle through the approach), so the inflation budget has to SHRINK, not grow.  The two fixes
are not independent, and this round measured the sign of the interaction by breaking it.

So the launch file is back to 0.099 and carries the whole budget beside the parameter, and **the map cannot
deliver the C2 row's 0.20 m footprint.**

=== WHAT IS LEFT, AND IT IS NOT THE MAP =======================================================
  * `optimization/lambda_fitness` (1.0) is the only optimiser weight that pulls the B-spline onto the A* path;
    `lambda_collision` (0.5) and `dist0` (0.5) multiply the SWARM separation term, which is empty for a single
    vehicle (`bspline_optimizer.cpp:962-1001`), so raising them is a no-op.
  * `check_collision_and_rebound()` checks only the first 2/3 of a trajectory (`i_end = end_idx -
    (end_idx - order_)/3`) and, when its rebound A* fails, DROPS the segment and keeps the colliding trajectory
    (`:1405-1408`) -- a hard check with a soft failure mode.
  * and the honest alternative: state that the C2 row's 0.20 m footprint is not reachable by this planner at this
    map resolution, with the measurement above as the reason, rather than keep tuning until a lucky run passes.

The section above explains DIAG12's 1 030 645 `a star error` as "two cells closes this field for ego's search".
THAT EXPLANATION IS REFUTED BY MEASUREMENT, and the correction matters because it reopens the lever it closed.

  * **THE CHANNEL WAS FREE.**  Measured from DIAG12's own DIAG/2 slices (`_stage/channel-width.py`), along the

    **median 41 of 41 samples free** (0.82 m), with **6% of saddles closed (fewer than 0.10 m)**.  DIAG9 at one
    cell measures **the same 41 of 41 and the same 6%**.  The channel did not close; the two runs' free space is
    indistinguishable by this measurement.
  * **THE RELAY DID NOT CRASH.**  `ego_relay.log` ends with a traceback in every run, including the ones that
    worked (`DIAG8`, `DIAG9`): it is `rclpy.executors.ExternalShutdownException` at shutdown.  DIAG12's relay
    published throughout -- **1544 scans, 23.7 M -> 11.1 M points, union 1.40x the newest scan**.
  * **WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED.**  The vehicle reached **0.579 m** at t=40.8 s, 1.8 m from the pad, and came down;
    from t~83 s it sat at L(-1.40, +1.10) at **z = -0.02 m for the remaining 370 s**.  The DIAG/2 slices at that
    position are **10201 of 10201 cells '?' -- the entire window is OUTSIDE the map box**, with occasional
    slices just inside as z crossed the boundary.  The map's z origin IS `grid_map/ground_height = -0.02`, so
    `isInMap` fails there; `getInflateOccupancy` returns **-1**; and `check_collision_and_rebound` reads -1 as
    **OCCUPIED** (`bool occ = grid_map_->getInflateOccupancy(...)`, `bspline_optimizer.cpp:1329`).  Hence
    **1 058 710** `the drone is in obstacle. This should not happen.` and an FSM that never planned again:
    `GEN_NEW_TRAJ` **19 049** against `EXEC_TRAJ` **207**.  A landed vehicle is outside its own map, and its own
    position then reads as an obstacle -- a deadlock, and nothing to do with inflation.
  * **WHAT IS WITHDRAWN**: "two cells collapses the search", "two cells is worse than three because the window
    thickens the map", and the conclusion "there is no value between 1 and 2 cells, so the map cannot supply the
    C2 footprint".  One cell is measured to work and two cells is measured not to, but the reason is a
    z-boundary deadlock, so **the inflation budget is NOT established as exhausted** -- only as untested above
    one cell under conditions where the vehicle stays airborne.
  * **WHAT IS NOT EXPLAINED**: why the vehicle descended in the first place.  It flew 1.8 m to within 0.10 m of
    a pillar SURFACE and then lost its trajectory; whether the two-cell map made the first plan take it there,
    or the adapter's behaviour after a plan it could not extend brought it down, is not measured.
  * **AND A STANDING DEFECT FOR THE WHOLE STACK**: `getInflateOccupancy` returning -1 outside the map, read as
    `true` by the collision check, means any ego sortie in which the vehicle touches down with the planner
    running can deadlock this way.  Figure `docs/figures/traj-xy-DIAG12-inflate020.png`.

=== SECOND CORRECTION: CLAIM (a) OF THE CORRECTION ABOVE IS ITSELF UNSOUND =====================
The correction above says "the channel was free" in DIAG12, on the strength of `_stage/channel-width.py`.  That
tool read the DIAG/2 slices with `free = not occupied`, and **a `?` -- the character `getInflateOccupancy`
returns as -1 for a cell outside the map box -- is not `1`, so it counted as FREE**.  DIAG12's slices were
mostly all-`?` (the vehicle sat at the map's own ground plane), so the tool reported a wide-open corridor that
was never observed.  This is the THIRD instrument error in this investigation and the second of the same
species: **"no data" read as a value.**  The tool now refuses to measure a slice that is not fully inside the
map, and refuses to measure one that is not at a height a plan would use (0.30 <= z <= 3.00 m).

Re-measured that way:

    DIAG12 (2 cells)       368      **4**                41 of 41 (median)    0 of 196

**DIAG12 HAS ONLY FOUR VALID SLICES** -- it was airborne and inside its own map for a few seconds -- so it
**cannot answer the corridor question at all**.  The honest statement about two cells is therefore neither row
79's "the channel closed" nor row 80's "the channel was free": **it is UNTESTED**, and the four valid slices are

WHAT SURVIVES FROM THE CORRECTION ABOVE, unchanged and independently measured: the **z-boundary deadlock** --
the vehicle at z = -0.02 m, which IS the map's z origin (`grid_map/ground_height`), outside its own map; the
slices at that position all-`?`; `getInflateOccupancy` returning -1; the collision check reading -1 as OCCUPIED
