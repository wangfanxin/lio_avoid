`C2-ego` is the goal's own criterion: >= 95 % of truth samples at >= 0.10 m from every pillar surface **and** 0
samples inside a body.  With a minimum of **+0.168 m**, 100 % of the 98 323 samples satisfy the first half and
none violates the second.  **This is the first time in this investigation that the aircraft has flown the
diagonal field without entering a pillar**, and it is the first run whose C2-ego verdict is MET.

The other rows are unmoved where they should be: A1 4.92 mm (<= 20), A2 1.97 mm (<= 50), C1 0.014 m from the last
commanded point.  The harness also prints `*** WENT THROUGH A PILLAR ***` on this run -- that line is its verdict
on the OLD 0.20 m footprint row (894 of 54 107 samples inside 0.20 m), not a penetration, and the acceptance row
for the ego arm was restated on 2026-10-16 precisely because this planner cannot hold 0.20 m in a 0.81 m field
(`docs/metrics/c2-prime-channel-20261016.txt`).

WHAT IS ATTRIBUTABLE TO WHAT (the arm had four parts, and the earlier flights separate them)
  * the two plan-side changes made in Flight B (the A* clearance cost and the whole-spline acceptance test)
    REGRESSED it there -- 148 plans with negative clearance -- and removing them is the single biggest
    difference between B and C;
  * the tracking-side changes were already measured good in B (reference max step 0.9945 -> 0.3576 m, tracking
    error p95 0.514 -> 0.356, max 0.922 -> 0.392 m);
  * the small reference (0.8 m/s / 1.0 m/s²) additionally puts the demanded speed into the band where the
    tracking error is 0.047 m, i.e. inside the plan's own 5-17 cm margin;
  * and the plan's own heading now reaches the FCU instead of being replaced by 0 (this is the first flight in
    which the aircraft turned toward its next waypoint rather than flying a fixed heading).
  So the honest statement is that the outcome changed when the two plan-side experiments came out and the
  reference was made small -- not that any single one of those four is sufficient on its own.

  * Nothing here says WTHe raised flight-controller limits (C1) helped: their own observable -- the FCU's internal
