      OBSERVABLE: the FCU's internal reference gap -- `flight.BIN` `PSCN` `_pos_desired` vs the wire -- whose

      PREDICTION: p90 falls below 0.10 m.  FALSIFIER: p90 stays above 0.20 m (then the shaper is not what was
      limiting, and the audit's 0.47 m attribution is wrong).

  C2  THE FSM CHECKS THE VEHICLE (code, `COLLISION-FIX/1` H1, `fsm/vehicle_collision_check` true)
      `checkCollisionCallback` now also tests `odom_pos_` against the map, airborne only (0.50 < z < 2.90 m),
      and re-commits from odometry (EMERGENCY_STOP if that fails), logging `VEHICLE-IN-OBSTACLE` at 1 Hz.
      OBSERVABLE: the count of `VEHICLE-IN-OBSTACLE` lines, plus whether any occurs while the aircraft is
      actually inside a pillar (truth).
      PREDICTION: at least one fires during any intrusion, i.e. the aircraft is NEVER inside a pillar for more
      than the 20 Hz check period without a log line.  FALSIFIER: an intrusion with the counter at zero.
      The risk this change carries: the map marks the vehicle itself (4346 `the drone is in obstacle` in the
      previous flight, 4165 of them during the climb), which is why the check is gated on being airborne.

  C3  THE SILENT COMMIT PATH IS TAGGED (code, H2)
      `SAFETY-RECOMMIT: ... this commit does NOT go through the 1 Hz gate` on the branch that re-commits without
      a word -- the branch the two sub-second commits before the last intrusion had to be attributed to BY
      ELIMINATION.
      OBSERVABLE: the count of `SAFETY-RECOMMIT` lines and the commit-gap distribution.
      PREDICTION: commits closer together than 1.0 s are now always explained by a log line.  FALSIFIER: a
      sub-second commit with no line naming its cause.

  C4  THE WHOLE SPLINE IS COLLISION-CHECKED (code, H3)
      `rebound_optimize`'s acceptance test now samples the entire duration (was `t < tmp * 2/3`) and
      `check_collision_and_rebound` scans to the last control point (was one third short).  12 of the 30 previous
      plans had their tightest point in that unchecked third.
      OBSERVABLE: the plan's own minimum clearance per committed plan (`traj.csv` `clr_m`), and `plan_success`.
      PREDICTION: the plan's minimum clearance rises above the previous +0.048 m and NO plan has a negative
      `clr_m`; the commit rate stays close to 1 Hz (the check must not starve planning).
      FALSIFIER: `clr_m` minimum unchanged or lower, or the commit count falls by more than half.

  C5  THE A* CLEARS THE BOUNDARY (code, `_stage/apply-clearance1.py` reapplied)
      A soft cost of 0.75 per occupied horizontal neighbour, so the search stops returning the SHORTEST path
      through a corridor -- the one that hugs its boundary.  Its single previous flight gave the best one-cell
      result of the series (-0.057 m) and was never reproduced.
      OBSERVABLE: the plan's clearance distribution (`clr_m` percentiles), which nothing else in this list moves.
      PREDICTION: `clr_m` 5th percentile above the previous +0.338 m.  FALSIFIER: unchanged, or the sortie
      aborts on the geofence as DIAG18 did (which was later attributed to a flyaway, so a repeat here is
      informative either way).

  C6  THE REFERENCE IS CLAMPED TO THE VEHICLE (code, `--ref-clamp-m 0.35`, `EGO_REF_CLAMP_M=0.35`)
      The contract shell -- the only process in the chain with the vehicle's state -- refuses to forward a
      reference more than 0.35 m from the aircraft, keeping the direction.  Removes the 0.281 m median /
      0.927 m max steps the sampler produces at every 1 Hz replan.
      OBSERVABLE: `shim_trace.csv`'s `jump_m` (the same column that measured 0.9945 m before), and the shell's
      own `REFERENCE CLAMPED` lines with the clamp count.
      PREDICTION: the pre-COMPLETED `jump_m` p99 falls below 0.10 m and no step exceeds 0.35 m.  FALSIFIER:
      p99 above 0.20 m, or the clamp firing on more than 30 % of the stream (which would mean the plan and the
      vehicle disagree so much that clamping is hiding a planning failure).

  C7  THE PLAN IS ASKED TO BE FEASIBLE (parameters: `EGO_MAX_VEL=1.2 EGO_MAX_ACC=2.5`)
      The measured tracking error is 0.047 m at 0.5-1.0 m/s and 0.346 m at 1.5-2.0 m/s, and the plan's own margin
      on the tight stretch is 5-17 cm; the plan was asking for up to 2.35 m/s.
      OBSERVABLE: the commanded-speed distribution and the per-speed-band tracking error.
      PREDICTION: the commanded speed never exceeds ~1.3 m/s and the tracking error at every band stays under
      0.15 m.  FALSIFIER: a commanded speed above 1.5 m/s (the soft penalties are not binding), or the mission
