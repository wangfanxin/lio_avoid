THE MEASURED CONSEQUENCE OF A+B, WHICH IS WHY THIS LIST MATTERS
    the parameter says a mark should reach ~0.399 m from a pillar's axis (surface 0.300 + one cell)
    PREDICTED outer mark with cell + cube diagonal: 0.540 m
    MEASURED, from the run's own occupancy slices: 0.540 m (median last mark; first mark 0.120 m)
    => the map's obstacle is 0.42 m thick on a 0.30 m radius, the free corridor between two pillars
       1.414 m apart is 0.334 m, and free space reaches 0.180 m INSIDE the pillar.
    With `obstacles_inflation` -> 0.0 the outer mark falls to 0.400 m and the corridor to 0.614 m;
    with `resolution` 0.05 + inflation 0.049 it is 0.419 m / 0.575 m (8x memory + the A* pool edit).
