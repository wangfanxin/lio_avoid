params/ego_planner_fast3.yaml raises ALL FOUR limits together (manager/max_vel 3.0, max_acc 3.0,
max_jerk 4.0, optimization/max_vel 3.0, bspline/limit_vel 3.0, limit_acc 3.0), because they are
coupled: limit_vel is the cap the refiner enforces, max_vel is what the control points are spaced by
in time, and at 1.0 m/s^2 the aircraft needs 3 s and 4.5 m to reach 3 m/s -- longer than these legs.
