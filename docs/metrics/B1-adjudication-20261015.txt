  * THE FAILURE IS AT THE TWO CORNERS ARRIVED AT FAST.  c2 and c4 are approached at 1.67-1.76 m/s and
    overshoot their wall by E +0.0610..+0.0817 m on every HOLD sortie; c3 is approached at 1.27-1.29
    m/s and overshoots by N +0.0065..+0.0143 m.  The E extent of the box is exactly this: c2's
    overshoot is the box's maximum and c1's return swing is its minimum.
  * IT RINGS AND THEN SETTLES -- IT IS NOT AN OFFSET.  On the four hold sorties the surviving fraction
    at the end of the window is 0.05-0.33 at c2 and c4 (RECT7 +0.0734 -> +0.0049, RECT10 +0.0817 ->
    +0.0067, RECT11 +0.0757 -> +0.0189), and the aircraft is back within 10 mm of the command 0.8-1.9 s
    after the peak.  A steady-state bias would not decay at all.
  * THE CONTROL FOR THAT IS THE SAME MEASUREMENT ON THE NO-HOLD SORTIES.  RECT1 and RECT6 arrive at the
    same 1.67-1.76 m/s and produce the same peak (E +0.0700..+0.0811 m), but with no dwell to settle in
    the surviving fraction is 0.90-1.00 and the 'back to 10 mm' column reads NONE.  Same ring, no time
    to decay -- which is why B1 reads 2.139-2.155 m on every sortie and why the fix is a dwell, not a
    bound.
  * AND THE SLOW ARM IS THE SCALE CONTROL: the same world and square with params/phase_a_mission.parm
    (WP_SPD 0.25) -- logs/missionA-stepB10-20261013_232331, 10 flights -- PASS B1 10 of 10 at
    E 2.024-2.060 / N 1.247-1.291 m against the SAME 2.10 / 1.30 bound, at about 7x lower arrival speed.

SO B1 STAYS FAIL, AND IT IS A REAL DEFECT OF THE FLIGHT, NOT OF THE WINDOW: the aircraft leaves the
commanded rectangle by 61-82 mm while arriving at corners it was commanded to hold, at an approach
speed (1.7 m/s) that params/phase_a_mission_fast.parm's own header says belongs to the 6.0 x 4.0 m
world.  The bound was not touched.
