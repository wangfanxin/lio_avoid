WHAT WAS CHANGED, AND THE MEASURED EFFECT OF EACH (all in `_stage/flight[F..J].sh`):

1. THE ARRIVAL GATE WAS SPHERICAL.  Flight F: "corner 1: closest horizontal approach 84.4 mm PASS, vertical
   +0.834 m" -- the aircraft stood 8 cm from the corner and 83 cm high, so a 3-D `d <= 0.35 m` gate never
   opened and the tour never advanced.  The gate is now `d_xy <= arrive_m` with a vertical TOLERANCE
   (`--arrive-z-m`), and `--settle-mps 0` removes the speed test: a 3 m/s aircraft spends ~0.2 s inside a

   EFFECT (Flight I, then J): eight arrivals out of eight, each logged at 0.325-0.349 m horizontal, at closing
   speeds 0.41-2.92 m/s.  Before: five arrivals and a stall.

4. THE REFERENCE IS NOW CONTINUOUS.  The sampler restarts `t_cur = now - start_time` at every 1 Hz re-plan, so

   rose; in Flight I it fires on 1.0 % of messages) and `--ref-slew-mps 6.0` bounds how FAST the forwarded point
