2. WHAT MOVED, WITH file:line
-----------------------------
* `planner/cmd_stream.py::TourPacer` is the PLAN box's executor: a WAYPOINT sequencer over
  `[c1,c2,c3,c4,c1]` (the entry leg has no dwell -- the tour's dwells belong to its corners), with
    - the arrival gate ESTIMATE-ONLY (`step(p_w, v_h, now)`: the caller's own odometry, nothing else);
    - **the dwell clock on the SETTLED arrival** (`_arrive`: inside `--arrive-m` AND below
      `--settle-speed`, both logged; `--settle-s` bounds the wait) -- F1 change 8;
    - `--leg-timeout-s` retained, names its reason and advances anyway;
    - the tour's end is a FIELD (`trajectory_flag = COMPLETED`) and the stream KEEPS publishing the hover.
* `planner/rectangle_source.py`: publishes `/plan/cmd` from its own `create_wall_timer` on a
  `MultiThreadedExecutor` (F1 change 1), steps the pacer from EVERY odometry sample (~200 Hz, against the
  FSM's 50 Hz), and NO LONGER PUBLISHES ANY `nav_msgs/Path`.
* `bridge/bridge_node.py` (NEW) `CmdRelay`: consumes `/plan/cmd`, checks `header.frame_id` (L, or ego's
  `world`), writes the newest sample through `send_cmd` at 50 Hz, counts the achieved rate, and names
  silence (`stalled_reason`) -- the fact that used to be indistinguishable from a completed tour.
* `bridge/mavlink_impl.py`: `send_cmd(cmd_l, calib, yaw_rad=None, fields="pos")` is THE ONE setpoint entry
  point.  **THE ENTRY POINTS THAT DIED** (F1 change 2): `send_position` (the corner tour's own writer),
  `send_position_velocity` (no live caller at HEAD 940cb50 -- the FSM's `posvel` arm fell through to
  `send_position`), `send_velocity` (only `--follow path`, deleted with the Path).  Their F/W frame
  contract is gone with them: nothing above the bridge converts a frame any more.
  `send_external_nav_l` closes the estimator path's L -> wire step the same way.
* `fsm/mission_fsm.py`: the corner walk is DELETED -- `_on_path`, `_path_in_hand`, `_legs_left`,
  `_await_next_leg`, `_fly_to_corner`, `legs`, `_corners_of` (F1 change 3), plus `follow_path` and
  `--follow path` with its nine switches (`--follow-mode`, `--lookahead-*`, `--brake-acc`, `--cross-gain`,
  `--traj-timeout-s`, `--traj-wait-s`), plus `--closed-m`, `--leg-join-m`, `--approach`, `--arrive-m`,
  `--settle-*`, `--next-leg-wait-s`.  1187 -> 869 lines.  The STALL GATE STAYS (design section 8 item 8)
  and now sits beside the command stream's own liveness gate.
* `tools/record-traj.py` reads `/plan/cmd` (F1 change 5, risk R5) with the same columns and the same
  meaning: `clr_m` is the clearance of the straight line the newest command implies, and the points file
  still holds one polyline per leg.  `tools/run-mission.sh` points it there.
* `tools/run-mission.sh`: `RECT_HOLD_S` defaults to 5.0 and prints where the dwell came from (change 7);
  `NEXT_LEG_ARG` (the two-clocks-are-one-number derivation) is retired with the interface it existed for.
* DELETED TOOLS, and why: `tools/test-corner-tour.py` (its subject, the FSM's corner walk, is gone -- its
  assertions moved into `tools/test-cmd-stream.py`), `tools/hold-design-check.py` (it decides between two
  Path ENCODINGS; there is no Path), `tools/test-shadow-ab.py` (it proves "the shadow stream does not move
  the flown Path"; there is no Path and no shadow).

5. WHAT THIS ROUND COULD NOT DO, AND WHAT REGRESSED
---------------------------------------------------
* **B1 still FAILS** and its bound was not touched (2.159/2.170 m East against <= 2.10).  It is STATUS
  row 50's defect and it is unchanged in size.
* **The `--nav-source truth` calibration flight was updated but NOT flown.**  `calibration_flight()` now
  builds its targets as an L `CmdSample` through `_manual_from_w` (the one place the FSM still converts a
  frame, and it must: that flight's purpose is to MEASURE the conversion), and the setpoint goes through
  `send_cmd`.  No sortie this round used it, so it is code-reviewed but not flight-proven.
* **`--stall-topic` still has no publisher.**  The gate is retained (design section 8 item 8) and its
  successor -- `CmdRelay.stalled_reason`, which fires after `--cmd-silence-abort-s` of silence on
  `/plan/cmd` -- was added and is exercised by the negative controls of `tools/test-cmd-stream.py`'s
  sibling logic, but NO SORTIE HAS YET KILLED THE PLAN BOX MID-FLIGHT.  That is F2's bench test.
* **`tools/measure-rates.py`'s setpoint row reads `t_wall` as if it were microseconds** (it looks for a
  `t_us` column that `setpoints.csv` has never had), so its "setpoint 84" number is meaningless; the honest
  number is the median interval of the `t_wall` column (48.31/48.43 Hz above).  Not fixed here: it is a
  measuring-tool defect, not an architecture one, and it reproduces on every pre-F1 run.
* **`bridge/mavros_impl.py` does not exist** and still does not (design section 8 item 11).  `bridge/base.py`
  no longer repeats the false claim; `docs/PLAN.md:34` and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md:225` still describe it in
  the conditional, which is the honest form until a round retracts it.


6. WHERE THE DESIGN DOCUMENT AND THE CODE DISAGREED
---------------------------------------------------
1. **The mask.**  `_stage/refactor-bridge-plan.md` section 3.3 prices the interface as "position, velocity,
   acceleration, yaw, yaw_dot -> msg 84 with the existing POS_VEL_ACC_YAW_MASK".  The CODE and the
   measurement say that mask is the one thing that BROKE holding (sortie G2: pos=(3.98,0) vel=(0,0)
   acc=(0,0) at the plan's end and the aircraft flew on at 2.6 m/s for 2.6 m until the geofence abort,
   against 0.020-0.023 m of stopping error on POS_YAW).  And at HEAD 940cb50 the corner tour's `posvel`
   default FELL THROUGH to `send_position`, i.e. it flew POS_YAW, so "the existing mask" was not what flew
   either.  Implemented: `send_cmd(..., fields="pos")` by default -- the mask the evidence was measured at
   -- with `posvel`/`posvelacc` selectable and documented.
2. **The stream's frame.**  Section 6 step S3 says "publish `/plan/cmd` (PositionCommand, **W**)" while
   section 3.3 says L.  Implemented: **L** (the same discrepancy S3 recorded and resolved; F1 confirmed it
   by making the bridge the only converter).
3. **`--empty-mark-s` / "stop publishing after COMPLETED".**  S3 findings section 1.4 left a consumer that
   needs a continuous stream to F1/F2.  Implemented: the stream publishes the hover FOREVER after
   COMPLETED, because "finished" and "stopped talking" must be different facts for the supervisor.
4. **The dwell clock.**  The design's section 6 F1 does not mention the settle at all; the F1 brief's
   change 8 does, with two measured misses behind it.  The code follows the brief.
