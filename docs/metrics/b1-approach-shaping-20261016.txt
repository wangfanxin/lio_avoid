APPROACH SHAPING, FLOWN: WHAT PLAN-SIDE MEANS DO TO B1, AND WHAT THEY CANNOT DO
================================================================================
Written 2026-10-15/16 by the round that flew the experiment.  Commit `17df0f2` is the feature (default
OFF); the sortie is `RECT39-shaping`; the attribution tool is the one `39218f5` added
(`tools/b1-edge-budget.py`), and every number below is that tool's or the run's own output.  The bound is
2.10 / 1.30 m and DID NOT MOVE.


1. THE EXPERIMENT
-----------------
`--approach-shaping-m 0.30` inserts ONE waypoint 0.30 m short of each tour corner, ON the leg, whose dwell
is ZERO: the vehicle must come to a SETTLED stop there (the settle gate is untouched -- the stop is what
makes it stop, not the dwell), so the last approach into the corner starts from rest.  Only legs longer
than 3 x D are split, so the 0.64 m pad->corner-1 transit keeps its geometry.  The tour becomes 9 waypoints
(the plan box's own start-up line: "APPROACH SHAPING: 0.300 m before every tour leg longer than 0.900 m --
4 waypoints added (dwell 0.0 at each: stopped at, not held), tour now 9 waypoints"), plus the climb leg =
10 targets, and the phase log reads `... leg1 .. leg10 ...`.

WHY THIS WAS THE CANDIDATE: B1's box is made by the under-damped ring that braking into a corner excites
(`docs/metrics/b1-mechanism-20261016.txt`): the aircraft crosses the corner at 0-5 mm, swings 99-142 mm
INSIDE, comes back, overshoots 35-93 mm PAST, and BOTH extremes are at ~0 m/s -- it stops out there.  Every
wire row commands position with velocity and acceleration exactly 0.0000, so the approach is a position
step and the plan's only lever on the ring is the SHAPE of that step.  The prediction's anchor was
measured, not assumed: the one leg this mission already flies from rest -- the 0.64 m entry from the pad --
overshoots 2.0-6.0 mm (its excursion column in `_stage/b1-arrival-shape.py`).

3. WHY IT STOPPED TEN MILLIMETRES SHORT (measured, and it is a property of the loop)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The corner-4 trace (`_stage/trace-corner.py`, every 0.2 s) shows the mechanism the shaping does NOT remove:
the plan declares arrival at the shaping point while the vehicle is STILL SWINGING around it, and the final
0.3 m move therefore starts from a moving, offset state:

  t 58.60  cmd (0.80, 1.60) [the shaping point]   truth E 0.8353  v 0.178 m/s
  t 58.80  cmd (0.50, 1.60) [the corner, NOW]     truth E 0.8287  v 0.089 m/s   <- the plan advanced
  t 59.40  cmd (0.50, 1.60)                       truth E 0.9261  v 0.036 m/s   <- 126 mm AWAY from it
  t 61.40  cmd (0.50, 1.60)                       truth E 0.5001  v 0.177 m/s   <- crosses the corner
  t 62.20  cmd (0.50, 1.60)                       truth E 0.4314  v 0.013 m/s   <- stops 68.7 mm PAST
  t 66.61  (dwell)                                truth E 0.4958                <- back inside, +4.2 mm

The plan's arrival rule is "inside 0.060 m of the commanded point AND slower than 0.080 m/s", and on a ring
those two are satisfied near the CENTRE of a swing, i.e. exactly when the vehicle is moving -- and the ring
at the shaping point then carries it up to 126 mm away before the corner move even starts.  This is the
same ring, excited one waypoint earlier: shaping MOVES the excitation, it does not damp it.  The plan has
no lever on damping: it commands positions only (velocity and acceleration are 0.0000 on every wire row),
so the ring's amplitude -- measured at 40-90 mm past a wall with the excitation, 2-6 mm without it -- is
the floor this configuration can reach at the corners it stops at.


4. THE TWO CANDIDATE TRADE-OFFS (no bound changed; neither is applied)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
(a) PLAN-SIDE, taken further: requalify the stop.  The vehicle must be at rest WITHIN A TIGHTER RADIUS than
    0.060 m before the final approach is commanded (a per-waypoint arrival gate for the shaped points), so
    the ring decays before the corner move starts.  Cost, measured: the ring at c4 took 4.4 s to come back
    inside the wall, and a 0.020 m gate would spend that time at FOUR waypoints per tour -- the tour's 67.7 s
    would grow by roughly 10-20 s, and the dwell/A4 rows would have to be re-verified because the hover is
    unchanged but the arrival instants move.  Expected benefit, from the same data: the final move would
    start from a settled state, which is the 2-6 mm case, so E growth would fall to ~30-50 mm -- a PASS with
    margin.  NOT TESTED: it is a new experiment, not an extrapolation this file can claim.
(b) FCU-SIDE, damping the loop: the slow arm already passes.  `params/phase_a_mission.parm` (WP_SPD 0.25,
    LOIT_ACC 0.3) reports B1 growth +38.4 mm E / +55.0 mm N, PASS, on the same square with the same
    estimator (stepB10-01, in the attribution table of `b1-mechanism-20261016.txt`), and its extremes are no
    longer corner events at all (0.065-0.771 m from the nearest corner, 0.04-0.32 m/s).  Cost: the route
    takes longer, every B1/B2/B3 and F1/F2 row must be re-verified, and a loop-damping change
    (PSC_XY/VELXY -- no params/ file currently overrides them) needs a bench step-response measurement
    BEFORE a sortie, plus a deliberate re-stamp of the frozen set and its parameter provenance.
BOTH change how the mission is flown; neither changes the bound, and NEITHER IS A FIX FOR THE RING -- they
are the two measured ways to keep the ring small enough for a 0.10 m allowance.


5. TWO RECORDED MISTAKES OF THIS ROUND
--------------------------------------
(i) `RECT36-ratio`'s harness died in POST-processing: "error reading input file: No data available",
    because `tools/run-mission.sh` was edited WHILE bash was reading it (the revision line and the
    `SHAPING_M` block were added during that sortie).  The FLIGHT is complete -- 11 phases ending in `done`,
    TOUR COMPLETE, no timeout, no abort -- and only the metrics step was lost, plus three orphan processes
    that `stop_stack` never got to stop (killed by PID after printing their own command lines:
    `_stage/clean-rect36-orphans.sh`).  `metrics.txt` was then produced by running exactly the command line
    `run-mission.sh` runs (`_stage/recover-rect36.sh`) and its rows are in the table above.  THE RULE: a
    file a running flight reads is not edited until that flight is over.
(ii) The stored `RECT34-accept/metrics.txt` still carries the OLD window rule's number (E 2.584 / N 1.651):
    it was written by the tool as it stood during that flight, and the window rule changed afterwards
    (commit `b92efed`).  The current tool reads the same run as E 2.163 / N 1.304, and BOTH numbers are in
    this file and in `f2b-climb-flight-20261015.txt`.  A sortie's own `metrics.txt` is left as the flight
    wrote it on purpose -- rewriting history is worse than a stated discrepancy.
