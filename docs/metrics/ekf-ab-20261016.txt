THE EKF A/B: THE VELOCITY NOISE AND THE TIGHT POSITION NOISE, ONE VARIABLE PER SORTIE
=========================================================================================
Written 2026-10-16 by the round that closed items 3 and 4 of the Mission A brief.  Every number is read
from the sortie's own `metrics.txt` (or, where stated, recomputed by the current tool from that sortie's own
logs); the parameter in force is read back from the FCU's own PARM records by `tools/bin-params.py`, never
from what we passed on the command line.  Figure `docs/figures/ekf-ab.png`.


1. THE INSTRUMENT THIS NEEDED FIRST
-----------------------------------
A4 was a POSITION row.  The velocity reaches the EKF on a different channel with different statistics, so
the only honest way to judge a velocity-noise change was to measure it.  `tools/metrics.py` now reads the
FCU's own log twice -- **XKF1 VN/VE/VD** (the estimate) and **VISV VX/VY/VZ** (the stream the EKF was fed,
documented North-East-Down in `AP_VisualOdom/LogStructure.h:69-79`) -- and prints both against the truth on
the clock A4's position fit already established, plus each stream's own best-fit lag, because the two lags
differ by ~110 ms and that difference is a delay a gate would otherwise score as an error.

THE VELOCITY STREAM, measured (`docs/metrics/velocity-stair-20261016.txt`): 46.96-47.11 Hz carrying
**10.01 Hz of real information**, 79 % of samples HELD (median hold 5 samples = 112.5 ms), steps at p90
125.7-138.8 and max 271.6-282.2 mm/s.  A4-v then measures its error against the truth: **68.8-83.8 mm/s rms
as consumed**, and **23.8-25.8 mm/s at its own lag of -104..-130 ms**.  `VISO_VEL_M_NSE` in force was
**0.0300** (read back from the FCU).

3. THE TWO DECISIONS THE BRIEF ASKED FOR
----------------------------------------
**(3) THE VELOCITY STAIRCASE IS TREATED BY SETTING THE PARAMETER HONESTLY, WITH THE MEASUREMENT.**  The
value is **0.069 m/s**, the lower end of the as-consumed error of the stream the FCU is actually fed, and the
effect is measured on the FCU's own velocity estimate: 31.75-38.28 -> 20.73-21.49 mm/s.  The alternative
reading (0.024 m/s, the same stream's error once its own ~110 ms of staleness is removed) was NOT chosen,
because that reading assumes the EKF compensates the delay; the as-consumed number is the conservative one,
and the flight that distinguishes them is the A arm flown here.  What the parameter still cannot express is
the CORRELATION -- five identical samples presented as five independent measurements -- and the note in
`velocity-stair-20261016.txt` says so.

**(4) THE TIGHT EKF CONFIGURATION IS PROMOTED, AS THE COMBINATION.**  `params/ekf_trust_lio_measured.parm`
is a byte-for-byte copy of `params/ekf_trust_lio_tight.parm` except for `VISO_VEL_M_NSE 0.03 -> 0.069`
(diff of the effective lines: one line), because the A/B shows the two changes are complementary and only
the pair reaches 7.68-8.04 mm.  **THE FROZEN SET WAS REVISED AS A DELIBERATE ACT**: the new file was added
with `tools/frozen-check.py --update params/ekf_trust_lio_measured.parm`, which prints what it stamped, and
`params/ekf_trust_lio.parm` and `params/ekf_trust_lio_tight.parm` are BYTE-FOR-BYTE UNCHANGED -- every
accepted row that names them still names the file it flew.  The locked acceptance baseline (RECT23/24/26)
was flown on `ekf_trust_lio.parm` and KEEPS that provenance; the promoted file's own three sorties are on
the record beside it.
